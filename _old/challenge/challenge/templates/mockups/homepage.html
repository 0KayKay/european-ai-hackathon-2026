The Ultimate Test of Wits

The AIxCHANGE Prompt Injection Challenge is a pre-conference event designed to test your creativity and problem-solving skills against a secure AI model. Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is to craft clever prompts that bypass its safeguards and extract a hidden secret key.

This is more than just a competition; it's a chance to explore the frontiers of AI safety, learn advanced prompting techniques, and connect with fellow enthusiasts before the main event.
